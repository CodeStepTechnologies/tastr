What is Tastr?

Tastr is a next-generation delivery platform built with students at its core and designed for everyone. Starting with food delivery, Tastr brings smart ordering, group orders, verified student discounts, and flexible delivery options — all in one seamless experience.

The platform is built to scale across food, grocery, alcohol, health & care, and more.

Our Vision

We’re making food delivery smarter, fairer, and more affordable for students across the UK and India. Our platform combines cutting-edge technology with student-first features to create an unparalleled dining experience.
